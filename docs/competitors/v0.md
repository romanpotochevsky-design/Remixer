# v0

> v0.app (Vercel) · pure AI builder · **best funnel, best agent self-verification**
> Detail: [`audits/synthesis-q3-2026.md`](audits/synthesis-q3-2026.md) §4.4

## What it is

Vercel's AI generator for UI and full apps, sitting directly on the Vercel platform. It is a
developer product with a designer's front door. Billing is credit-based at close to raw token
rates: Free gives $5 of credits **and** a hard 7 messages/day, and the next rung is **$30/user/mo**
— there is no individual paid plan between them, the $20 Premium tier having been sunsetted
`[verified]`. The model ladder has a **40× output-price spread** (Mini $1.20 vs Max Fast $50 per 1 M
output tokens), and **v0 Max is the default for a new anonymous user** — the second-dearest tier
`[verified]`.

**Registrar: Vercel sells domains** inside the publish flow (Publish → Customize → "Buy a Domain")
at registrar cost, recently cut by up to 50 % `[verified]`.

## Why it matters to Remixer

Two dimensions where we score worst, and v0 defines the state of the art on both.

**The funnel.** There is no wall at all before generation: a logged-out visitor typed "a simple
landing page for a coffee shop" and received a complete, styled, multi-section, self-QA'd page —
verified by direct run. The wall arrives *after* the payoff, as a modal whose second clause,
*"or duplicate this chat"*, converts loss aversion directly (§4.4) `[verified]`. We score 3 on
onboarding and the audit's Tier 1 item #4 is about not losing the prompt through SSO (§7).

**The wait.** We score **2/5 on generation legibility** (§2). v0 is the only agent that visually QAs
itself in public, and its step rows are the template for the choreography in Tier 1 item #5.

## Steal

- **Declare an art direction out loud before writing code.** A step row "Generated design
  direction", then a sentence in plain design language: *"I'll commit to a warm, editorial
  craft-coffee direction with a deep espresso palette and amber accents."* (§4.4) `[verified]`.
- **Write theme colours and font tokens *first*, before any component**, and show it in the step
  list — "Updated theme colors" → "Added font tokens" → "Set up fonts and metadata" → components
  (§4.4) `[verified]`. This is an anti-generic mechanism that costs no extra wait, unlike asking the
  user to pick between three directions up front.
- **The agent screenshots its own work** at desktop and mobile and posts the thumbnails into chat,
  with the literal shell line rendered as a step. It found a real defect in v0's own starter
  component and chased a false-positive console error to ground (§4.4) `[verified]`. Dead waiting
  becomes trust (§5.2).
- **Design Mode attaches a screenshot of the selected element to your natural-language
  instruction** — automatically. Plus a Before/After comparison toggle and ⌘I to switch between
  inspect and interact. Free on the free tier (§4.4) `[verified]`.
- **The closing summary names design decisions in design vocabulary** — the invented brand name, the
  typeface pairing, the palette, the semantic tokens it wrote (§4.4) `[verified]`.
- **Empty states that teach**, with shortcuts rendered as actual keycaps rather than prose (§4.4)
  `[verified]`.
- **Design Systems 2.0**: a design system imported as a *skill* from repos, Figma node URLs,
  Storybook or npm packages, emitting a manifest, running a verification chat that **pauses for
  human approval before saving**, and thereafter refusing to use components or tokens it cannot
  verify. Updating a skill deliberately does not rewrite existing projects (§4.4, §5.2)
  `[verified]`. This is the strongest answer in the field to "who arbitrates when the agent's
  aesthetic conflicts with a saved design system" (§10.2 Q26).

## Traps

- **Six minutes and twenty-one seconds to first render**, badged "Worked for 6m 21s", with the
  preview pane showing only *"Your v0 generation will show here."* the entire time (§4.4)
  `[verified]`. Self-verification is not free — it is paid for in wall-clock time.
- **The self-healing is billable.** Roughly a third of the visible steps in the measured run were
  spent fixing an incompatibility in **v0's own starter Button component**. One user measured ~20 %
  of iterations as corrections for v0-caused breakage (§4.4) `[verified]`. Lovable and GoDaddy both
  repair their own errors for free; v0 charges.
- **Every builder toolbar icon ships with `aria-label` and `title` both null** — only "Add to
  favorites" carried a label (§4.4) `[verified]`. A measured accessibility failure in the product
  the whole field treats as the craft benchmark for developers.
- **Design Mode is unavailable on read-only chats and mobile viewports**, so the visual editing story
  does not extend to the iOS app or to shared links (§4.4) `[verified]`.
- **The dearest model is the default for a stranger.** On a ladder with a 40× spread, that is a
  billing decision dressed as a product decision.
- **"Built with v0" badge on free deployments** (§4.4) `[verified]`.

## Domain / publish behaviour

Buys in-flow and auto-configures what it sells `[verified]`. For anything else, **external-domain
DNS is punted entirely to the Vercel dashboard** — no record types, no verification UI, no guidance
in v0's own docs (§4.4) `[verified]`. And what the user finds there is the category's canonical
anti-pattern: Vercel's status vocabulary is `Valid Configuration` / **`Invalid Configuration`** /
`Pending Verification`, which names the machine's opinion rather than the user's next move, and pays
for it in community threads titled *"Domain Showing 'Invalid Configuration' Despite Correct Setup"*
that run for years (`connect.md` §5.2) `[verified]`. Ownership is proven by a `_vercel` TXT record;
apex additions auto-suggest the www counterpart; existing CAA records need
`0 issue "letsencrypt.org"` added by hand (`connect.md` §7, §8, §6 row 6) `[verified]`. A domain
previously attached to a Lovable project — which runs on Vercel underneath — **cannot be added to
another project until it is fully released**, and the user cannot even see what is holding it: a
dead end for a novice (`connect.md` §9) `[likely]`. **No email product at any tier** (§8.2)
`[verified]`.

## Do not repeat

- **"We are faster than v0."** The comparison is n=1 against an internal marketing figure, across
  different output classes — v0's 6m21s run included image generation, browser QA and screenshot
  verification, and we admit we do not know our own p50/p90 (critique §2, §10.2 Q2). The audit leads
  §6 with "Ahead" on a number it flags as unknown eleven pages later. Do not inherit that.
- **The credit-burn quotes as measurements.** The community complaints are real and consistently
  directioned, but "$30 in a single day" is one user's report, not a benchmark.
- **v0 as evidence for per-message cost receipts in *our* product.** v0's and Lovable's audiences are
  technical and cost-curious; putting a price tag on every message teaches a novice that talking is
  expensive and suppresses the iteration our retention depends on. This recommendation sits in direct
  tension with reducing credit anxiety — pick one (critique §4.8).
