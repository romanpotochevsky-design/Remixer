# Raw research dumps — archived, not curated

These three JSON files are the **raw output of the multi-agent competitor research run of
August 2026**. They are machine-written dumps, not knowledge. Nothing here has been edited,
deduplicated, reconciled against the other files, or checked for staleness.

**Do not cite from these files.** Everything in them that survived verification has already
been promoted into the curated documents — chiefly
`docs/competitors/audits/synthesis-q3-2026.md` (with its corrections applied inline) and
`docs/competitors/audits/synthesis-critique.md`, which lists the claims that did *not*
survive. Where a dump and a curated document disagree, the curated document wins.

They are kept for one reason only: **provenance.** When a line in the audit needs its
original source, wording, or confidence tag traced back, it is in here.

| File | What it holds |
|---|---|
| `recon_dossiers.json` | 6 competitor dossiers — Lovable, Bolt.new, Base44, GoDaddy Airo, v0, plus one "category sweep" covering ~15 adjacent players. 420 findings, 746 verbatim microcopy snippets, 355 source URLs, each dossier also carrying positioning, summary, standout UX, weaknesses and 2026 pricing. |
| `lenses.json` | 6 cross-cutting UX lenses — onboarding & time-to-wow, builder shell & IA, the chat/agent editing model, publish/domains/hosting/SSL/email, monetisation UX, visual design & motion craft. Each holds an executive take, a competitor comparison, best-in-class picks, adopt/avoid/leapfrog lists and open questions. |
| `factcheck_corrections.json` | 10 adversarial fact-check corrections — 5 verdicts `refuted`, 5 `unverifiable` — each with the original claim, the correction and a source URL. These are the corrections already folded into the audit. |
